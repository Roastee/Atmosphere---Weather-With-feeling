# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, constructive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use the project; and
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered professional misconduct; or
- other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may take any action they consider appropriate and proportionate in response to behavior they consider unacceptable, including warnings, removal of contributions, temporary or permanent bans, or other corrective action.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards fairly and consistently, protect the privacy of reporters where possible, and explain decisions when doing so does not create additional harm.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. If no private channel is listed, open a minimal issue asking maintainers how to report it privately; do not include sensitive details in that issue.

Security vulnerabilities must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. It may also apply outside project spaces when conduct has a material impact on the safety or ability of others to participate in the project.
